About

I am a statistician working at the intersection of quantile regression and conformal prediction. My research designs estimators that remain reliable under heteroscedasticity and produce prediction intervals with finite-sample, distribution-free coverage.

I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics at Korea University under Dr. Yoonsuh Jung, and now continue this work as a postdoctoral researcher under the mentorship of Dr. Haeun Moon.
